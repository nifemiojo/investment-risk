## Teaching Strategy: Practice-First

**Other approaches:**
- Formula dump (here's VaR = Mean - Z*Vol) → forget it
- Theory first (normal distributions, percentiles) → abstract
- Production first (show you Spreadex code) → black box

**Our approach:**
1. **Build it** — Working calculator on real data
2. **Play with it** — Modify parameters, see results change
3. **Break it** — Find scenarios where VaR fails
4. **Understand it** — Extract principles from what we built
5. **Apply it** — Connect to your job, your interviews

**Why this works:** You learn by doing, not passively absorbing.
